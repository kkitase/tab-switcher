Switch Chrome tabs the same way you switch windows with Alt+Tab.

■ How to use
・Press Option+Q (Alt+Q on Windows) to show your tabs in most-recently-used order, with the previous tab selected.
・Keep holding Option and press Q again to move to the next tab. Release Option to switch to the selected tab.
・A quick single press takes you straight back to the previous tab, so you can go back and forth between two tabs.
・You can also click a row to switch. Press Esc or click outside the list to close it without switching.

■ Features
・Tabs from all open windows are listed. Choosing a tab in another window brings that window to the front.
・The list appears in the center of the current page. On pages where extensions cannot run, such as chrome:// pages, a small window opens in the center instead.
・You can change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
・Supports both light and dark mode.

■ Privacy
Tab titles and URLs are used only inside your browser to show the list. Nothing is sent to any external server.
